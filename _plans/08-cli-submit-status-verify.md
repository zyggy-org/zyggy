## Step 1 — A prompt and a target become a signed, round-trip-checked job envelope, or a named refusal: `JobRequest.TryCreate` refuses every bad `to`, `project`, `agent`, `timeout_minutes`, `allowed_tools[i]`, `priority` and `body` shape with a `JobRequestError(Field, Detail)` and accepts registry names, POSIX and Windows paths, a `Bash(dotnet *)` tool item and a body starting with `---`; `JobSubmitter.BuildAsync` signs with `signWith`, serialises, re-parses and re-verifies the exact bytes, compares every field and the body byte for byte, and answers `Built(envelope, bytes)` or `BuildRefused(field, detail)` — no checkout involved

## Assumptions (where the spec leaves a shape to the planner or an implementation choice is needed; each is reviewed at the named gate)

1. **A1 *(Gate A)* — the "substituted emitter"**: `FrontMatterEmitter` is static, so `JobSubmitter` carries an `internal Func<Envelope, byte[]> Serializer { get; init; } = EnvelopeWriter.Serialize` that one unit test replaces to break the round trip; production never sets it.
